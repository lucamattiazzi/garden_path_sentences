# Research scope: garden-path sentences only

This repository studies surprisal at the actual disambiguating token and causal
circuits involved in processing garden-path sentences. For `The old man the boat`,
keep `man` (ambiguous word), the second `the` (disambiguating word), and `boat`
(spillover/continuation) distinct. Do not replace this question with general
agent/patient classification, logical analysis or role-transfer benchmarks.

Keep every new behavior or intervention tied to the same temporary ambiguity and
matched clearer controls. Surprisal, continuation preference, a generated parse
and causal evidence for a circuit are different claims. Do not describe a layer
maximum or final-readout state copy as discovery of a comprehension circuit.

The original V3 GPS bridge and V4 GPS-context gate are historical auxiliary checks.
Simple-sentence helper code needed to reproduce them is a dependency, not a new
research track. Retained source/metadata snapshots are immutable provenance, even
when their historic contents include formerly shared code. General-role branches
were removed on 13 October 2026; do not restore them without an explicit request.

Preserve raw results, frozen protocols, checkpoint revisions and negative results.
Use fresh run IDs, prespecify comparisons, separate development from confirmation,
and report lexical/position/length confounds. A hand-picked title example is an
exploratory case study, not evidence of generalization or a discovered circuit.
